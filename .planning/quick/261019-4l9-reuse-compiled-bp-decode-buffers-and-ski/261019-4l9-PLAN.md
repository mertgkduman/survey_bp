# Reduce compiled plain BP decode overhead

Goal: measure and reduce avoidable allocation and marginal work in the optional compiled binary and BP4 decode prototypes, with unchanged public sweep snapshots and decoder semantics.

1. Capture a same-host, pinned-core baseline for warmed binary SPA/min-sum and BP4 decode calls on the existing BB72 and GB46 seeded workloads.
2. Reuse private per-decode scratch and message buffers across iterations, and omit BP4 marginal normalization only in the plain decode path. Keep public `sweep` output complete and independent.
3. Run existing differential tests and matched post-change timings. Record output identities, speedup or regression, JIT/setup costs, and remaining optimization opportunities. Keep these prototypes outside publication execution and budget/ledger integration.
