It requires the complete parent audit, exact regeneration of training-only proposals, seed/selection locks and all frozen per-row/reference/endpoint checks. Eight allocated audit CPUs process independent points; they do not generate a new result cohort. The remaining three live beam runs are excluded rather than interpreting their interim stopping prefixes as final estimates.

Comparisons use each prespecified budget winner and actual measured confirmation latency. A budget is an upper constraint, not a claim of exact equality. Different hosts' absolute time is not compared directly; contemporaneous same-CPU OSD-0 reference ratios are retained. Pointwise20-failure uncertainty, missing OSD references and incomplete points remain explicit. Interval overlap is not equivalence; full-grid counts are descriptive rather than a simultaneous superiority claim. Per-point controls can differ across rates, so nonmonotonic estimates do not identify a physical threshold.
