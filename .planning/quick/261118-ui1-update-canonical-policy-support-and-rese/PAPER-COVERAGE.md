# Paper figure coverage audit

The retained PriorTilt campaign reproduces the comparisons in [PriorTilt Fig. 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.13373v1) with canonical adaptations of PriorTilt, RelayBP and BeamSearch. Its 39 X/Z-aggregated points are not automatically reproductions of the figures in the original RelayBP or BeamSearch papers.

## RelayBP

[RelayBP](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.01779v2) Figure 1 is an algorithm illustration. Figure 2 has interval heatmaps and logical-error curves for gross, two-gross, and distance-11 surface-code circuit memories. Figure 3 compares logical error versus mean iterations for several BP, memory and Relay configurations on gross. Figure 4 is a graph illustration. The public catalog's BB72 controlled CSS comparison is not a reproduction of these figures. Reproducing their data requires the original circuit models, rate grids, memory-interval grid, control methods, number-of-solutions and leg budgets, and statistical trials. Existing PriorTilt Figure 1 gross results use different paper settings and do not establish Figure 2 or 3 coverage.

## BeamSearch

[BeamSearch](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.07057v2) Figure 1 illustrates its algorithm. Figures 2–4 report circuit-level results for BB144, BB90 and HGP450, including parameterized search configurations and baseline decoders. Tables 2 and 3 report mean and tail runtimes. The BB72 CSS study and PriorTilt Fig. 1 use different experiment specifications. Reproduction requires each original circuit, noise point and beam configuration, matching baseline and logical-outcome semantics, and separate qualified timing measurements. The PriorTilt paper's BeamSearch results are retained under PriorTilt.

## BP-SF

[BP-SF](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.00254v1) Figure 1 analyzes iteration behavior, Figure 2 studies oscillation candidate selection, and Figure 3 is an algorithm diagram. Figures 4 and 5 show code-capacity results for coprime-BB [[154,6,16]] and BB [[288,12,18]]. Figures 6–8 show circuit-level results for BB [[144,12,12]] and coprime-BB [[154,6,16]] and [[126,12,10]]. Figure 9 compares serial iteration work versus error at p=0.003. The BB72 factorial controlled comparison is not a source figure reproduction. Missing work includes the source code and circuits, noise grids, source-specific oscillation/flip selection and sampling, baseline implementations, work budgets and enough terminal samples for each source curve. The Figure 7 surrounding text names a different code than its caption, so source clarification is needed before treating that assignment as established.
