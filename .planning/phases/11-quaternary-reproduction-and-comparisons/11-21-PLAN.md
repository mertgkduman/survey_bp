## ASVS Level and Enforcement

OWASP ASVS L1 applies. Any unresolved high or critical threat blocks plan completion and leaves the production proposal denied.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| scheduler/site output → qualification parser | Site commands and administrator text are external inputs that can be stale, malformed, or refer to a different account/partition. |
| reviewed targets/manifests → proposal builder | Scientific artifacts must be joined by canonical digest rather than mutable path or display name. |
| representative measurements → forecast | Bounded profiles cannot be extrapolated across unmatched code, decoder, environment, or workload identities. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-11-21-01 | Spoofing | Site capability identity | high | mitigate | Bind hostname/cluster, exact account and partition, collection commands, timestamps, and evidence digests; deny on ambiguity or mismatch. |
| T-11-21-02 | Tampering | Target/manifest join | high | mitigate | Recompute canonical digests at proposal time and reject path-only or label-only identity. |
| T-11-21-03 | Repudiation | Resource/cost provenance | medium | mitigate | Persist source, collection time, command/result digest, and explicit absent-field reasons. |
| T-11-21-04 | Information Disclosure | Scheduler output | medium | mitigate | Store only allocation facts needed for authorization; redact unrelated users, job payloads, and credentials. |
| T-11-21-05 | Denial of Service | Representative profiling | high | mitigate | Enforce the existing bounded profile caps and never call a production submission path from qualification. |
| T-11-21-06 | Elevation of Privilege | Proposal interpreted as authorization | high | mitigate | Give the proposal a non-authorizing schema and require a separate exact authorization receipt in Plan 22. |
</threat_model>
