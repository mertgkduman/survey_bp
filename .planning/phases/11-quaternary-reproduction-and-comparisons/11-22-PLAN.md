## ASVS Level and Enforcement

OWASP ASVS L1 applies. Any unresolved high or critical threat blocks receipt materialization; the effective decision remains deny.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| proposal → readiness router → human decision | A digest-bound package reaches a human authorization decision only after the complete readiness conjunction passes. |
| human decision → receipt materializer | The selected scope must be represented exactly without inferred or widened authority. |
| stored receipt → dispatch entry point | A persisted token is untrusted until reloaded, rehashed, checked for expiry, and matched to live artifacts/site limits. |
