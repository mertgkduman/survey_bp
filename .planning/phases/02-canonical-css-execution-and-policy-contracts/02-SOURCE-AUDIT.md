## Prohibition recall / precision

The recall pass over all eleven requirements produced routine correctness concerns, canonical security concerns, and bespoke scientific-integrity concerns. The precision pass:

- Drops routine engineering items already covered by edge probes/tests.
- Refers path traversal, arbitrary imports, environment leakage, and evidence tampering to the per-plan ASVS threat models rather than minting duplicate prohibitions.
- Keeps two bespoke transparency constraints as descriptor-less flagged-unverified `must_haves.prohibitions`: diagnostic profile selection must not become a population estimate (Plan 07), and development evidence/provisional novelty must not become reproduction or established novelty claims (Plans 08–09).

No `check_*` descriptor is fabricated.

## Exclusions (not gaps)

- Full CPU/GPU profiler-adapter coverage and production GPU optimization are deferred by `02-CONTEXT.md`.
- Source-faithful RelayBP, BPGD, impulse, branching/search, additional schedules, and fallback algorithms remain in later roadmap phases; Phase 2 implements only their typed lifecycle/evidence expressibility.
- Publication-scale statistical targets, compute quotas, and retention are later preregistration/campaign decisions; Phase 2 defaults are explicitly development-only.

All GOAL, REQ, in-scope RESEARCH, and D-01–D-26 CONTEXT items are `COVERED`. There are no unplanned source items and no phase split is required.
