## How to use the register

- **Potential contribution** states the narrow method or artifact, not a superlative. It must be traceable to executable evidence and tests.
- **Closest precedent** records a cited or explicitly incomplete comparison. “No verified closest precedent yet” means unresolved search, not novelty.
- **Unresolved search** is carried forward into the mapping study and must be refreshed before an abstract, manuscript, or review asserts differentiation.
- **Claim maturity** may advance only after corpus recheck, precise comparison, and evidence appropriate to the proposed claim. Development output is not assessed reproduction, population performance, or established methodological novelty.

## Locked evidence limitations

Profiling and selective tracing are deliberately listed as provisional. A representative paired cohort can support a specified profiling estimate only when selection precedes outcomes, inclusion probability is retained, and semantic agreement is established. Targeted diagnostic replays and bounded traces remain explanatory evidence, not population evidence. The canonical three-qubit development result is canonical implementation evidence, not a source-faithful reproduction or a statistical reproduction claim.

Before manuscript use, re-run the unresolved search against the expanded literature corpus, record the exact compared implementations and definitions, and downgrade or remove any statement that lacks a defensible closest precedent.
