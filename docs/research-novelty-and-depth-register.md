## Cross-kernel memory question for later phases

The proposed study asks whether a **specified memory or relay mechanism** changes logical outcome, convergence, and work in the same way under separate binary CSS inference and joint quaternary Pauli inference on matched physical samples. Phase 4 establishes and probes memory policies in the binary CSS kernel. Phase 10 must derive and verify any mathematically compatible four-state counterpart; Phase 11 owns matched joint-versus-separate code-capacity comparisons. Phase 13 addresses Relay-BP's original circuit setting. A shared policy name is not evidence that the binary scalar recurrence, quaternary vector update, and circuit error-mode graph have the same semantics.

The [Relay-BP paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.01779) defines its circuit decoding problem with binary error-mode variables, a check matrix, an action matrix, and independent mode priors after graph construction. Its XZ and XYZ circuit decoding strategies differ in which binary decoding objects they use. **Inference:** even when XYZ decoding retains cross-type circuit information, it does not constitute a quaternary Pauli BP kernel with a joint I/X/Y/Z variable posterior. A quaternary memory extension therefore needs its own equation, normalization and state-transfer semantics, compatibility proof or explicit adaptation label, and independent Y/correlation fixtures.

Before promoting a claim, the project must identify the closest published quaternary memory and relay methods, prespecify matched samples and budgets, retain negative and inconclusive results, and distinguish a mathematical policy extension from an empirical observation about its effect. This entry tracks a candidate research contribution; it does not assert priority or improved performance.

## How to use the register

- **Potential contribution** states the narrow method or artifact, not a superlative. It must be traceable to executable evidence and tests.
- **Closest precedent** records a cited or explicitly incomplete comparison. “No verified closest precedent yet” means unresolved search, not novelty.
- **Unresolved search** is carried forward into the mapping study and must be refreshed before an abstract, manuscript, or review asserts differentiation.
- **Claim maturity** may advance only after corpus recheck, precise comparison, and evidence appropriate to the proposed claim. Development output is not assessed reproduction, population performance, or established methodological novelty.

## Locked evidence limitations

Profiling and selective tracing are deliberately listed as provisional. A representative paired cohort can support a specified profiling estimate only when selection precedes outcomes, inclusion probability is retained, and semantic agreement is established. Targeted diagnostic replays and bounded traces remain explanatory evidence, not population evidence. The canonical three-qubit development result is canonical implementation evidence, not a source-faithful reproduction or a statistical reproduction claim.

Before manuscript use, re-run the unresolved search against the expanded literature corpus, record the exact compared implementations and definitions, and downgrade or remove any statement that lacks a defensible closest precedent.
