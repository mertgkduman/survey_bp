A `.spec.toml` is a frozen, non-runnable source interpretation; a `.toml` is a qualified runnable canonical adaptation. Target, manifest, and assessment files are separate because they answer different questions. Missing entries are explicit; a paper row does not imply source-faithful reproduction.
